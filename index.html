The office

Your workday has rooms in it.

Nothing is buried in a settings list. Everything you do lives somewhere in the building, and you tap the place to go there.

Ground floor · Reception

Badge in and out

Two taps a day. The hours add themselves up, and the desk keeps a record of every shift.

Ground floor · Front door

End the day

Walk out and the clock stops. Lunch comes off automatically, on the schedule you set.

Top floor · Workstation

Sit down and focus

A 25-minute session at the desk. It keeps running if you leave the app, and it remembers where it got to.

Top floor · Printer

Print a pay slip

Hours, rate and total for a shift, printed once and never rewritten by a later change of pay.

The printer

Every shift comes off the roll.

A pay slip is a snapshot, not a formula. Change your rate next month and last month's paper still says what it said.

  • Per-day hours, because Monday and Friday don't have to match
  • Its own lunch break on every day of the week
  • Paid by the hour, month or year, in your own currency
